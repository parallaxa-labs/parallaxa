The first public release of **Parallaxa**, a free OBS Studio plugin that gives 2D VTuber scenes depth, camera moves and effects.

## What it does

- **Depth and parallax.** Give each layer of your scene (sky, background, avatar, foreground) a depth. As the camera moves, every layer shifts at its own speed, so a flat 2D scene feels like a small stage.
- **Virtual camera.** 15 camera moves (zoom, pan, punch-in, shake, nod and more), saved camera views with smooth, whip or cut transitions, and an auto camera that drifts or reacts to talk and music. Everything has hotkeys.
- **Looks and effects in one pass.** 10 FX presets and 3 LUTs: colour looks, bloom, grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. Add per-layer depth blur with rack focus between your avatar and the background.
- **14 ready-made recipes.** One click sets the camera, the look and the depth of field for a stream type, such as Cozy Chat.
- **Parallaxa dock.** Control layers, depth, camera, views and looks from an OBS dock.
- **Sample scene.** Use **Tools > Parallaxa: Import sample scene** for a ready-made demo scene with original art.
- **For apps.** An obs-websocket vendor API (`parallaxa`, protocol 1) lets other apps drive the camera and looks. The protocol docs are MIT-licensed.

It is light on the GPU: about 0.2 to 0.3 ms per frame in our tests, with no skipped frames.

## Install

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and OBS Studio 32.0 or later (33.x supported).

1. Close OBS.
2. Download **Parallaxa-0.1.0-Setup.exe** below and run it. The installer finds your OBS, including the Steam version.
   - Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because this installer is new and not yet code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Open OBS. Look for **Parallaxa** in the Tools menu and the Docks menu.

If you prefer the zip, extract **Parallaxa-0.1.0-windows-x64.zip** into `C:\ProgramData\obs-studio\plugins\`.

## Files

- `Parallaxa-0.1.0-Setup.exe`: the Windows installer.
- `Parallaxa-0.1.0-windows-x64.zip`: the plugin, for manual install.
- `Parallaxa-0.1.0-pdb.zip`: debug symbols, for crash reports.
- `Parallaxa-0.1.0-source.zip`: the complete corresponding source (GPL-2.0-or-later).
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums.
